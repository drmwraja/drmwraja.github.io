About Me

Academic background, scientific career and research leadership

Dr. Mir Wasim Raja is a Principal Scientist in the Energy Materials and Devices Division (EMDD) at CSIR–Central Glass and Ceramic Research Institute (CGCRI), Kolkata. He obtained his M.Sc. in Physical Chemistry from the University of Burdwan and his Ph.D. from Jadavpur University, Kolkata, where his doctoral research focused on the development of high-performance electrode materials for lithium-ion batteries under the DRDO missile programme.

Before joining CSIR-CGCRI, he served as Assistant Manager (R&D) at UNTPL, NEI Corporation, USA, working on nanostructured electrode materials. From 2012 to 2020, he served as Senior Scientific Officer in the Chemistry Division of the State Forensic Science Laboratory (SFSL), Government of West Bengal, Kolkata. During his tenure at State Forensic HQ, he established and headed the Crime Scene Management Division (CSMD), investigating more than 250 road traffic accident cases and approximately 850 crime scenes.

His forensic-science work included pioneering methods for electronic data extraction and analysis from vehicles, enabling scientific reconstruction of road accidents and vehicle-related crimes. He also served as Guest Faculty at LNJN-NICFS, Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India, and was recognized as a national-level trainer for safety and failure investigations involving high-end and autonomous vehicles.

His research contributions include engineered microstructures for high-performance battery materials, paper-based ceramic separators and advanced energy-storage materials. He has published more than 30 research papers and holds six patents related to battery materials and separators. One of his notable innovations is PAPERATOR, a paper-based ceramic separator technology developed for Li-ion/Na-ion batteries and supercapacitors.

From materials innovation to clean energy solutions.

Research contributions span the development of advanced electrode materials, sustainable separators, solid-state electrolytes, and next-generation battery architectures, with a focus on translating materials innovation into practical energy-storage technologies. Key contributions include nanostructured Li-ion electrode materials, the PAPERATOR® paper-based ceramic separator, engineered LLZO solid electrolytes, monolithic all-solid-state battery architectures, and biomass-derived functional carbon materials. This integrated approach combines materials engineering, interface design, sustainability, and cell-level development to advance safer, high-performance and scalable energy-storage solutions.

Research leadership

Dr. Wasim currently serves as Principal Investigator of the DST-MECSP Mission programme Energy Storage Platform on Batteries (ESPoB). Since 2024, he has led two CSIR, Government of India Mission programmes as Principal Investigator: the Waste to Wealth Mission and the Redox Flow Mission.

He also serves as Lead Principal Investigator of the DST, Government of India-sponsored Next Energy Storage Technologies (NEST) Mission, with current research focused on innovative solid-state batteries (SSBs) and fiber-shaped energy storage devices (FSESDs) for next-generation energy applications.
