About Me

Scientific profile and research vision

I am a Principal Scientist at CSIR–Central Glass & Ceramic Research Institute (CSIR-CGCRI), Kolkata, working in the Energy Materials & Devices Division (EMDD). My research focuses on advanced materials for energy storage and conversion, including solid-state batteries, redox-flow batteries, ceramic electrolytes, functional membranes, and electrochemical characterization.

My research approach integrates materials design, ceramic processing, electrochemical evaluation, spectroscopy and device engineering to translate materials innovation into practical energy technologies.
