About

Dr. Mir Wasim Raja is a Principal Scientist at the CSIR–Central Glass & Ceramic Research Institute (CSIR-CGCRI), Kolkata, India, where he leads research in advanced materials for electrochemical energy storage. His research encompasses all-solid-state lithium batteries, fast lithium-ion conducting ceramic solid electrolytes, interface engineering, and sustainable energy technologies. Through multidisciplinary research, he develops innovative materials and scalable battery technologies, including paper-based ceramic separators (PAPERATOR) and redox flow battery systems, bridging fundamental materials science with practical engineering solutions. His contributions have led to high-impact scientific publications, patented technologies, and nationally funded collaborative research programmes, advancing the development of safe, high-performance, and sustainable energy storage systems for future electric mobility and grid-scale energy applications.
